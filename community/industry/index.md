With over twenty years in the High Performance Computing (HPC) sector, EPCC as part of the ARCHER2 team was committed to bringing the world of HPC to all industrial and commercial sectors. With a range of initiatives, members of the team could work with commercial partners to help overcome any barriers they had using HPC. 

EPCC had a long history of working alongside academia, industrial and commercial partners on world leading HPC. Research collaborations could take many forms and were funded through a variety of mechanisms that included the UK Research Councils, Scottish Enterprise and European Commission.

Work was undertaken across a varied range of sectors including aeronautical, engineering, manufacturing, astronomy, retail, finance, medical, agricultural, oil and gas. 

There were a variety of methods for commercial organisations to gain access to ARCHER2, whether as part of a research collaboration or working within a larger established consortia or even asking for assistance on a consultancy basis. 

Commercial organisations wishing to make use of ARCHER2 could get in touch and their interest would be directed appropriately.
