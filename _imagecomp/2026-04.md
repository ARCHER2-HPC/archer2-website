title: Picture Showing the Synthesis of Ammonia on Manganese Nitride (Mn3N2)
description: There is significant recent interest to investigate potential routes for ammonia synthesis is gas phase under plasma conditions.  We have previously calculated the barriers for dinitrogen hydrogenation in gas phase and found that they are prohibitively high in the absence of a catalyst. However, the use of energetic electrons to produce radial cations in dinitrogen may result in the synthesis compounds under energetically more efficient conditions. This picture depicts on a hypothetical basis the reactants and products of such as gas phase reaction, in which the molecules are enclosed in their corresponding van der Waals surfaces.
