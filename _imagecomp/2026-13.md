title: High-resolution atmospheric dynamics over Southern Africa
description: The video shows the evolution of potential vorticity, a measure combining local wind rotation and atmospheric profile stability, on a 300 hectopascal pressure surface. The output is from a high-resolution "convective-scale" simulation of the atmosphere over Southern and Eastern Africa, run using the Met Office's Unified Model on ARCHER2. Large swirls in the south are Rossby waves propagating into the domain&colon; these dominate variability in weather over Southern Africa. To the north, over central Africa, small-scale dipoles of potential vorticity erupt and coalesce into large, organised thunderstorms which propagate for days at a time and bring rain across the continent.  Later, these small dipoles become embedded in the larger Rossby wave structures, forming cloud bands which bring rainfall on large scales across Southern Africa.
