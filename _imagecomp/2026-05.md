title: Large Eddy Simulation of Ignition and Flame Propagation in a Lean-Direct-Injection Hydrogen Combustor 
description: Hydrogen is increasingly recognised as a critical component of future energy systems with reduced carbon emissions. However, safely and efficiently using hydrogen in engines and turbines remains challenging due to its high reactivity and distinct combustion characteristics compared to traditional fuels. By computationally modelling combustion, engineers can test ideas virtually without relying on costly or risky real-world experiments. This animation is from an ARCHER2 Pioneer Project carried out by researchers from the University of Cambridge investigating the use of hydrogen as a low-carbon fuel.
