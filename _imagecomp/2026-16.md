title: β-amyloid 42 oligomer unfolds and blocks the α-7 nicotinic acetylcholine receptor
description: This video shows a molecular dynamics simulation of a beta-amyloid 42 oligomer unfolding and blocking an alpha-7 nicotinic acetylcholine receptor (pink) found on neuronal membranes. The oligomer is in the form of a tetramer (4, 42 amino acid chains in red, yellow, blue and green) and loses its beta-sheet structure becoming jumbled, sticky and yarn-like. It spontaneously attracts and binds to the surface of the receptor, blocking entry to neurotransmitters, cell signalling and normal conformational changes to open and close. This behaviour may explain the synaptic loss and neural degeneration found in neurodegenerative diseases like Alzheimer's Disease. The simulation was conducted using GROMACS on ARCHER2, and VMD was used for video capture and rendering.
