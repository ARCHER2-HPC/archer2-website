## Contacting the ARCHER2 Service Desk

The ARCHER2 Service Desk was the first point of contact for all questions relating to the ARCHER2 Service. Support was available Monday to Friday from 08:00 until 18:00 UK time, excluding UK public holidays. The ARCHER2 Service Desk could be reached by:

- [Email support@archer2.ac.uk](mailto:support@archer2.ac.uk)
- [Via the SAFE](https://safe.epcc.ed.ac.uk/)

Users were asked to provide as much background information as possible as this sped up the processing time considerably. Existing ARCHER2 users were asked for their user ID, project code, and, where applicable, the commands they were using and the error message they received. In order to investigate issues, users could be asked to share data (e.g. source code, job scripts, input data) with the service team. Information on how to share data [could be found in the ARCHER2 documentation](https://docs.archer2.ac.uk/user-guide/data/#sharing-data-with-all-archer2-users).

For more complex queries a callback could be booked to discuss issues with an EPCC expert at a time of convenience. This could be done via video call or telephone.

## About the ARCHER2 Service Desk

The service desk was available to prospective and current users of the Service. It was available between the hours of 0800 and 1800, Monday–Friday (excluding UK Bank Holidays). Users typically contacted the service desk via email, but users could also submit a query via the SAFE. 

The Service Desk was staffed by experienced members of the ARCHER2 Service team who could assess the nature of the issue and then either deal with the issue directly or assign it to an appropriate member of staff. The team aimed to personally acknowledge queries within 3 hours and—along with the acknowledgement of the query—assigned a service level to the query and an estimated resolution time, which was communicated to the user. Queries were designated either as SP or CSE queries and then further designated as Level 1, 2, and 3 queries, depending on the estimated complexity. Each query metric had a different Service Level Agreement which was reported on a quarterly basis. 

The Service Desk was the face of the ARCHER2 service to the user community and the team valued the personal interactions with the users. When dealing with queries, the team ensured polite, timely communications whilst working to resolve the issues. Once the query was resolved, the Service Desk asked the user for feedback on the handling of the query. Feedback was consistently high: if there was an identified issue, this was followed up with the user. 


## How the Service Desk query system works

When a query was submitted by email or through the SAFE, it was placed in the SAFE query system. Phone queries were entered into the system by the Service Desk operator.

When users emailed their query, they normally received an automatic email response, including a tracking ID, within a few minutes. If a query was submitted through SAFE, a tracking ID was provided at once. A few minutes later, the query was assigned to the appropriate expert within the ARCHER2 team to handle.  A personalised acknowledgement email was sent, letting the user know how their query was classified, the expected resolution time and any other information which was immediately available.

The expert could contact the user to discuss their problem or to get extra information. Finally, the expert sent an answer, and the query was closed. At this point the Service Desk sent another message, telling the user that this had happened.

Closed queries were kept in the SAFE database, so that the team could refer back to them when solving future problems, and when writing documentation, etc. The SAFE database was protected by the [ARCHER2 Personal Data and Privacy Policy](../about/policies/privacy.html).
