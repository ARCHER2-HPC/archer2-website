The ARCHER2 Service ended at 17:00 on Friday 20th November 2026.


<!--more-->

ARCHER2 ran for 5 years, from early access on the preliminary 4-cabinet system in December 2020 up to November 2026. Over that time, it has run almost 16 million individual scheduled jobs (not counting job arrays or individual job steps) using more than 125 million node hours (more than 16 billion core hours). It has been consistently heavily used - exceeding 90% utilisation for the majority of its lifetime, supported thousands of researchers and hundreds of research projects. We will be publishing a blog post in the future taking a more detailed retrospective look at the statistics of the ARCHER2 service over its lifetime.

All of the ARCHER2 partners (EPSRC, NERC, EPCC, HPE and the University of Edinburgh) have worked hard to provide the best service we can over the lifetime of ARCHER2 and have particularly enjoyed working with users of the service and seeing the research it has enabled. We hope that you have had a good experience with the ARCHER2 service over the past 6 years and EPCC are collecting short notes on the research that use of ARCHER2 has enabled.
